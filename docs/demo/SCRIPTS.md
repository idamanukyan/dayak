# Dayak demo videos — narration scripts (for nannies / supply)

Three screen recordings of the live platform are in this folder:

| File | Shows | Language on screen |
|---|---|---|
| `nanny-onboarding.webm` | How a nanny joins and gets verified | Armenian |
| `parent-search-request.webm` | How families find and request you | Armenian |
| `admin-verify.webm` | The trust behind the "Verified" badge | English (admin console) |

These are raw product recordings (no audio). Record a voiceover over them, or add
on-screen captions. Scripts below are written to **recruit nannies** — the message is:
*free to join, easy to onboard, and real families are already looking for you.* Keep a
warm, respectful tone. Suggested length per clip: 40–60 seconds.

> Tip: the recordings have deliberate pauses so narration fits. Trim dead air in an
> editor (CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, or Canva) and add soft background music + your logo.

---

## 1. `nanny-onboarding.webm` — "Join in minutes, free"

**Hook (0–5s)** — "Looking for nanny work in Yerevan? With Dayak you build one trusted
profile — and families come to you."

**Sign up (5–15s)** — "Signing up is free. Choose 'Nanny', add your name and phone, and
you're in."

**Profile (15–25s)** — "Tell families what you do best — your districts, the ages you
love working with, your languages and your rate. You're in control of your terms."

**Interview (25–45s)** — "Instead of repeating yourself on call after call, you do one
short chat interview. Answer in your own words, in Armenian. You can pause and come back
any time — nothing is lost."

**Documents (45–55s)** — "Upload your ID and a selfie — kept private, seen only by the
Dayak team. Add two references and you're ready for review."

**Close (55–60s)** — "That's it. Free to join, done in minutes. Dayak does the
verifying — you get the families." → *Logo + "Join at dayak.am"*

## 2. `parent-search-request.webm` — "Families are already looking for you"

**Hook (0–5s)** — "This is what a parent sees — and why being verified on Dayak means
real work."

**The map (5–20s)** — "Families search a live map of Yerevan and filter by district,
language, child's age and schedule — exactly the details on your profile."

**Your card (20–35s)** — "Verified nannies show up with a trust badge, experience, and a
short summary. When you match what a family needs, you're right there."

**The request (35–50s)** — "The parent requests an introduction with one tap. Our
coordinator connects you — and a family only pays the matching fee after it works out."

**Close (50–60s)** — "The more complete and verified your profile, the more families
reach out. Build it once; get matched again and again." → *"Become a verified nanny —
dayak.am"*

## 3. `admin-verify.webm` — "Why 'Verified' means something"

> Use this clip to build trust (with nannies *and* parents). It shows that the badge is
> earned, which is exactly why families pay for a Dayak match.

**Hook (0–6s)** — "Every 'Verified' nanny on Dayak is checked by a real person. Here's
what that looks like behind the scenes."

**The review (6–30s)** — "A coordinator sees everything in one place: the interview
transcript and summary, the uploaded ID and selfie, and the reference calls. Documents
are reviewed one by one; references are called and logged."

**Verify (30–50s)** — "Only after the ID checks out, two references confirm, and an
in-person meeting is set, does the coordinator click Verify. That's the moment your
public profile goes live to families."

**Close (50–60s)** — "No shortcuts, no fake profiles. That's why families trust Dayak —
and why being verified is worth it for you." → *"dayak.am"*

## How this makes money (the honest version)

These videos are **recruitment + marketing**, not the revenue itself. The revenue is the
**5,000 ֏ matching fee** a parent pays once a match works out (already built into the
platform). The growth loop:

1. These clips recruit **nannies** → more verified supply.
2. More supply → better matches for **parents** → more paid matches.
3. Post the nanny clips in Armenian mom/nanny Facebook groups, Telegram channels, and
   with a `?start=src_fb1`-style deep link on the bot so you can see which channel brings
   the most sign-ups (sources are tracked in the admin metrics).

Fastest path to first revenue: get ~10–15 nannies verified (these clips help), then run
the parent clip to families in 2–3 target districts.
