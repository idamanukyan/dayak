# Dayak (D1) — 30-day validation plan, 28 Sep – 25 Oct 2026

Source: experiment F2 in the Armenia consumer-opportunities study. Team: Ida (Berlin, bot + sheet + metrics), sister (Yerevan, interviews, references, matching, Idram).

## Week 1 · 28 Sep–4 Oct · supply, nothing public
- Bot live (BotFather + host), Sheet connected, both of you as admins
- Sister joins the FB groups (admins take days), posts staff.am listing (HY + RU), nanny recruiting posts
- DM 10 pediatricians + 5 private kindergartens
- Interview 20–25 nannies in person; reference calls same/next day; ID photo
- Log every refusal and why (refused_id / no second reference / no answer)
- **Gate:** ≥15 vetted. <10 willing to be listed with references → stop (failure mode 2)

## Week 2 · 5–11 Oct · demand
- Post 1 (HY) + Post 2 (RU) day 1; boost 50,000 AMD split; Post 3 on day 5 with real numbers
- Every request called within 24 h, 1–2 nannies proposed, free meet, 5,000 AMD via Idram before trial day
- **Gate:** 30 requests · 10 matches · 5 paid

## Week 3 · 12–18 Oct · trust layer under load
- Trigger one backup case for 2 paying families (nanny "unavailable"), replacement within 4 h; ask if they'd pay for it as a standing feature
- Offer shared nanny to two same-district families
- Ask every paying parent for one referral; log source
- Day-14 call to every matched parent: still with the nanny? still through us?

## Week 4 · 19–25 Oct · decide
Continue if ALL: ≥5 paid · ≥2 parents ask about backup unprompted · ≥1 shared-nanny pair · referrals ≥0.3/paying family · no evidence nannies go around us after placement.
Otherwise: the parent list becomes the seed base for Mama Care (D2).

## Budget
Helper ~600k AMD · boost 50k · Idram business account · hosting ~€5 → ≈ €1,700

## Weekly metrics (pull from the sheet every Sunday)
requests / matches / paid · conversion request→paid · backup_importance split · source split · nannies interviewed / vetted / refused · median rate asked · time-to-first-call
